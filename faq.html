FAQ

Questions brands
ask us.

Do you offer OEM and private label manufacturing?

Yes. Pincraft works under three models: OEM manufacturing to your own designs and specifications, private label from our existing range of 36+ tweezer styles and allied products, and bulk supply for spas, salons and grooming kit manufacturers.

What products do you manufacture?

Tweezers (36 styles across 9 body types), blackhead removers (5 types), cuticle nippers, pushers and cutters, and gua sha tools for face, body and scalp. Our signature collections include Swarovski crystal (Solitaire), premium matt (Elegante) and etched-pattern (Bella) tweezers.

What materials and finishes do you use?

Our implements are made from 304 and 420 stainless steel — surgical-grade, corrosion-resistant and anti-magnetic. Finishes include steel, coloured glossy, coloured matt and finely-powdered matt, with gold PVD on request.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs depend on the product, finish and packaging you choose. Private label programmes and card-backing packaging are available with lower MOQs. Full MOQs are listed in our product catalog, or send us an enquiry with your volumes.

Can you customise designs and packaging?

Yes. We can customise crystal shade, body colour, etched pattern and finish, add logo engraving or embossing, and develop bespoke designs for brand exclusives. Packaging options include custom retail boxes, card backings and gua sha gift sets — we handle design, print and assembly.

Which countries do you export to?

We supply beauty brands and retailers in Europe (Italy, Spain, France and Sweden), the United States, the UAE and GCC, and Australia, and we are onboarding partners in new markets. OEM partnerships include global shipping and logistics.

Can I request samples before ordering?

Yes. Tell us about your project through the enquiry form or at info@pincraft.in and we'll come back with samples and a quote. We typically respond within one business day.
